## Overview

[Deepspace](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/ses-ai-launches-agentic-capability-latest-molecular-universe-release-increase-value) is a multi-agent scientific AI system for battery and materials scientists. Rather than relying on a single LLM, the system coordinates specialized agents, each responsible for a distinct subtask, and synthesizes their outputs into coherent, evidence-grounded responses to complex R&D questions.

The system draws on a large scientific literature corpus alongside a millions-scale molecular properties database spanning DFT-computed descriptors and ML-derived properties, grounding its reasoning in both experimental evidence and computed molecular data to support molecule discovery workflows.

---

## Contributions

**Agent Architecture Design**
Defined agent roles, instructions, tool use, and inter-agent interaction logic from the question decomposition stage through evidence synthesis to final response generation.

**Capability Roadmap**
Translated domain research needs into a structured expansion roadmap covering data analysis, visualization, literature-grounded reasoning, and domain-specific tool integration.

**Latency Optimization**
Diagnosed latency bottlenecks at both task and agent levels through systematic analysis, achieving a 6× reduction in end-to-end response time with minimal impact on output quality.

**Evaluation**
Established a systematic evaluation protocol against a prior baseline, which served as the foundation for ongoing model diagnosis and iterative capability development.
